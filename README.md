This repository contains the peer evaluation for **Practical Applications 1+2**. Both the repository structure and the 8-minute presentation have been designed to align with the official evaluation rubric (1–5 scale across 5 blocks).

### 1. Assessment Criteria Alignment
The project addresses the core requirements across all five rubric blocks:

* **Block A · Content & Rigour (45%)**
  * *Problem framing & research question (6%):* Explicit research question grounded in prior literature.
  * *Data, target & leakage control (6%):* Documented dataset source, size, predictors, target threshold, class balance, baseline, and strict data leakage prevention.
  * *Experimental design & validation (9%):* Comparison of non-probabilistic and probabilistic classifier families, feature-selection schemes, stratified cross-validation, and disclosure of optimistic bias sources.
  * *Results & evidence (9%):* Headline and class-wise metrics (Accuracy, AUC, Sensitivity, Specificity) compared against the baseline with uncertainty/significance estimates.
  * *Interpretation & insight (10%):* Rigorous statistical interpretation of model behavior (distinguishing odds vs. probabilities and association vs. causation).
  * *Conclusions & limitations (5%):* Evidence-backed conclusions directly answering the research question, alongside concrete limitations and next steps.
* **Block B · Story & Synthesis (15%)**
  * *Narrative arc & selection (8% + 7%):* Distilled main deck covering all assignment pillars (non-probabilistic, probabilistic, and feature selection) with one core message per slide.
* **Block C · Slides & Visuals (15%)**
  * *Legibility, figures & notation (8% + 7%):* Concise slide layout ($\le$ 80 words/slide), defined formulas, and properly labelled graphics with honest axes.
* **Block D · Delivery (15%)**
  * *Timing, oral delivery & audience fit (5% + 6% + 4%):* Structured for an 8-minute presentation slot with precise ML terminology.
* **Block E · Q&A & Backup (10–12%)**
  * *Answers & backup material (7% + 3–5%):* Indexed backup slides covering full metric tables, feature selection details, and methodology.

---

> **Peer Evaluation Statement:** The contribution percentages and task allocations above were mutually agreed upon by all team members following an internal peer review of code quality, experimental validation, and presentation delivery.
